As global supply chains evolve, Canada continues to stand out as a reliable and strategically positioned market for international trade. Its extensive transportation infrastructure, access to three oceans, and close integration with the United States give businesses several options for reaching customers across North America and beyond. For companies entering the Canadian market, this combination creates a stable environment for growth.
